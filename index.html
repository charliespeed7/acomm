What does being agent-first mean?

Nobody in your category is agent-ready today. That's the opportunity. None of this is a replatform. All of it is buildable in quarters. The rails are already live.

UCP
Universal Checkout Protocol
Google's agent checkout standard. Walmart and Target are already signed. Lets an agent complete a purchase end to end on your store without a human in the loop.
● Live
ACP
Agent Commerce Protocol
Stripe and OpenAI's Instant Checkout inside ChatGPT. An agent can browse, compare, and buy from a merchant in a single conversation. Already live for participating retailers.
● Live
FIDO
Agent Payment Mandates
The FIDO Alliance standard for agent-to-merchant payment authorisation. Verifies that an agent has permission to pay on behalf of a human. The trust layer for autonomous commerce.
● Live
01
Readable catalogue
Structured product data an agent can parse: price, stock, sizes, identifiers, policies. Adobe finds only about half of retail content is machine-readable today.
02
Live local inventory
Store-level stock visible to machines, so "get it by Friday near me" resolves to your stores, not a rival's.
03
Agent checkout
An agent completes a purchase end to end via UCP and ACP, starting with one category.
04
Instrumented funnel
Agent traffic, conversion and revenue reported monthly, next to paid and organic, against this model.
05
Governance
A deliberate policy on which agents may read and transact, how mandates are verified, and who owns the number.
